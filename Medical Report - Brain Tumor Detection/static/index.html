6. Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulations

A. Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) Equations:

  • Contrast: $\sum_{i,j} |i - j|^2 \cdot p(i, j)$
  • Dissimilarity: $\sum_{i,j} |i - j| \cdot p(i, j)$
  • Homogeneity: $\sum_{i,j} \frac{p(i, j)}{1 + |i - j|^2}$
  • Energy (ASM): $\sum_{i,j} p(i, j)^2$
  • Entropy: $-\sum_{i,j} p(i, j) \log_2(p(i, j))$

B. Otsu's Skull Stripping Threshold:

Maximizes between-class variance: $\sigma_b^2(t) = \omega_0(t)\omega_1(t)[\mu_0(t) - \mu_1(t)]^2$

C. Performance Evaluation Metrics:

  • Accuracy: $\frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN}$
  • Sensitivity (Recall / TPR): $\frac{TP}{TP + FN}$
  • Specificity (TNR): $\frac{TN}{TN + FP}$
  • Precision (PPV): $\frac{TP}{TP + FP}$
  • F1-Score: $2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$
